1. Company Name
LocalJobs India — Connecting local workers with local jobs

2. What We Do
LocalJobs is a hyperlocal job marketplace that connects workers with employers in Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities across India. Job seekers can find trusted jobs near them and apply instantly — no resume required, no fees, no middlemen.

3. Top 10 FAQs
Q1: How do I find local jobs near me?
A: Sign up as a worker on LocalJobs, complete your profile, and browse jobs in your city. You can filter by area, job type, salary, and distance (2km–20km). Apply with one tap — no resume needed.

Q2: Is LocalJobs free for workers?
A: Yes, 100% free. Creating a profile, browsing jobs, applying, and connecting with employers costs nothing. No hidden fees.

Q3: Is it free for employers to post jobs?
A: Yes, employers can sign up, get verified, and post unlimited job listings at zero cost.

Q4: What types of jobs are available?
A: Delivery, Driver, Helper, Mechanic, Electrician, Plumber, Cook, Waiter, Cleaner, Security Guard, Sales, and Factory Worker positions across India.

Q5: Do I need a resume to apply?
A: No! Just complete your profile with your skills, experience, and availability. You can apply to any job with a single tap.

Q6: How does verification work?
A: Workers verify via Aadhaar document upload (OCR-processed). Employers verify with business documents (GST/PAN/License). Admin reviews and approves all profiles before they go live.

Q7: How long does verification take?
A: Typically within 24 hours. Once approved, you'll get full access to the platform.

Q8: How do I contact an employer after applying?
A: Once your application is shortlisted/selected, verified contact details are shared so you can connect directly with the employer — no middlemen.

Q9: What languages does LocalJobs support?
A: The platform supports Hindi, English, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and Punjabi.

Q10: How many jobs can I apply to per day?
A: Workers can apply to up to 10 jobs per day. Jobs remain active for 30 days after posting.
